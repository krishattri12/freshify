Shipping & Delivery

Last updated: July 2026

Because our smoothie bowls are made fresh to order, delivery works a little differently than standard shipping.

Delivery area

We currently deliver within a limited local radius to ensure bowls arrive fresh and cold. Enter your address at checkout to confirm whether you're in our delivery zone.

Delivery windows

Orders placed before 11am are typically delivered same-day. Orders placed after 11am are delivered the following day. Exact windows are confirmed at checkout.

Packaging

Bowls are packed in insulated, chilled packaging to keep them fresh in transit. We recommend refrigerating or enjoying your bowl within a few hours of delivery.

Delivery issues

If your order arrives damaged, melted, or incorrect, contact us within 24 hours at hello@freshify.example and we'll make it right with a replacement or refund.

Pickup

Local pickup may be available depending on location — this can be selected at checkout if offered in your area.
